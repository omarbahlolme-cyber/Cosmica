and agency of a high-end interactive studio with scientific-atlas clarity.

Galaxies, and a compact account icon at top-right. Exact lower-left title text:
'Cosmica Universe'; small supporting text 'Choose a galaxy.' Only a few subtle
physically anchored readable labels: 'Languages', 'Science', 'Computing'. Suggest
depth and a coherent camera path into the selected Languages galaxy through
composition, not a fake overlay diagram. No permanent panels, no boxed card grid,
no cockpit frame, no fake telemetry, no progress points, no gamification, no
marketing paragraphs, no ornamental button rows, no watermarks. This concept is
the universe entry screen; its logic leads Universe > Galaxy (discipline) > Star

The result must look like a polished usable original product, with breathtaking
scale and rigorous restraint.
