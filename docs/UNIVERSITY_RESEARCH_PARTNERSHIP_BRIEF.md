# Cosmica University Research Partnership Brief

## 1) Executive Summary

Cosmica is a multilingual learning platform with 12 language worlds (`English`, `French`, `German`, `Spanish`, `Portuguese`, `Dutch`, `Swedish`, `Arabic`, `Chinese`, `Japanese`, `Korean`, `Hindi`).  
We are seeking academic partnerships to independently evaluate and improve pedagogical quality, linguistic accuracy, learner outcomes, and human-AI instructional interactions.

Goal: produce publishable, evidence-based findings that validate what works, identify weaknesses, and guide measurable remediation.

## 2) Why Partner With Cosmica

1. Real applied environment: live product with real learners, not only lab simulation.
2. Multilingual scope: one platform, cross-language comparative research.
3. Controlled remediation loop: findings can be implemented quickly and re-tested.
4. Research traceability: versioned content and audit checkpoints allow before/after analysis.

## 3) Proposed Research Tracks

1. Pedagogical Effectiveness
- Question: Do map-based micro-lessons improve retention and transfer?
- Outputs: retention deltas, error reduction curves, completion behavior.

2. Linguistic Accuracy and Register Appropriateness
- Question: Are rules/examples/mistakes native-valid and context-correct?
- Outputs: per-world error taxonomy and correction recommendations.

3. UX and Cognitive Load
- Question: Does map structure reduce friction and improve focused practice?
- Outputs: task-completion time, confusion hotspots, dropout points.

4. AI Support Quality (Mimi Assistant)
- Question: Does guidance improve learner persistence and confidence?
- Outputs: intervention quality rubric and recommended prompting policy.

## 4) Methodology (Suggested)

1. Mixed-method design:
- Quantitative: completion, streak continuity, section revisit, correction success.
- Qualitative: professor rubric review, learner interviews, confusion annotation.

2. Sampling:
- Beginner-focused cohorts, optional intermediate subgroup.
- Per-language balanced review slices.

3. Evaluation windows:
- Baseline (current version), patched iteration(s), and post-remediation follow-up.

4. Deliverables:
- Interim findings memo (4-6 weeks),
- Final report with validated rubric and roadmap (8-12 weeks),
- Optional joint conference paper submission.

## 5) Data and Ethics

1. Data minimization: no unnecessary personal data collection.
2. Research access by role and need-to-know only.
3. Compliance-first workflow (consent, anonymization, retention policy).
4. Security controls aligned with production hardening practices.

## 6) Collaboration Model

1. Cosmica provides:
- Access to reviewed content snapshots,
- Technical logs/metrics exports (anonymized),
- Fast implementation cycles for tested recommendations.

2. University team provides:
- Linguistic and pedagogical expert review,
- Methodological design and validation standards,
- Independent analysis and publication guidance.

## 7) Requested Partner Profile

1. Applied linguistics / SLA faculty.
2. Language-specific experts across our 12 worlds.
3. Optional HCI / learning sciences collaborators.
