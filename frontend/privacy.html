Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cosmica collects and uses information when you use https://www.cosmicalearn.com.

Information We Collect

  • Information you submit in feedback forms (name, email, message).
  • Technical request metadata required for security and operations.
  • Aggregated analytics events used to improve site performance and reliability.

Mimi Chat Privacy

Mimi chat history is handled client-side for the current browsing session experience. We do not maintain an owner/admin dashboard to read individual user Mimi conversations. When you refresh the page or leave the homepage flow, the visible Mimi conversation state resets in the interface.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to feedback and support requests.
  • Operate and secure the platform.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve user experience.

Premium Billing and Payment Data

For premium subscriptions, payment processing is handled by trusted third-party payment providers. We do not store full payment card numbers in Cosmica systems. We store only the minimum subscription and billing state needed to provide premium access, support, reconciliation, and fraud prevention.

Cookies and Session Data

We use essential cookies/session tokens for authentication, account continuity, and security controls. These are required for core account and premium features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited data may be processed by trusted infrastructure/service providers strictly for hosting, analytics, and communication.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for service operations, security, contractual obligations, and legal compliance. Retention periods may vary by data type (for example, account records, security events, and billing events).

Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has submitted personal information through our services without appropriate consent, contact us and we will review and handle the request according to applicable law.

Security

We apply technical controls including encrypted transport (HTTPS), security headers, monitoring, and rate-limiting. No Internet system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Your Choices

To request deletion or correction of information submitted through feedback channels, contact us at admin@cosmicalearn.com.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy over time. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
