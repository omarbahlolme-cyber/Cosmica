Learn Japanese Kana

Kana

日本語かな

This foundation map starts with the core hiragana and katakana charts, then adds voiced sounds, contracted sounds, and the small っ / ッ cue used in real reading.

Write

Trace vowels and rows before speed

  • Write あいうえお first, then rebuild each consonant row until the pattern feels automatic.
  • Keep the kana shape smooth and balanced before trying to write quickly.
  • Read each row aloud as a rhythm unit instead of isolated symbols with long pauses.
Rhythm

Read small ya, yu, yo and small tsu as one beat

  • A small ゃ/ゅ/ょ joins the kana before it, so きゃ is one sound chunk, not two separate beats.
  • A small っ or ッ marks a doubled consonant and creates a short stop before the next kana.
  • Practice chunking first, then add pitch and speed after the rhythm is stable.
Decode

Move quickly from charts to real reading chunks

  • Use the chart only as a launchpad, then practice words with yoon, small tsu, and long vowels.
  • Read hiragana in whole rhythm groups like きょう and がっこう.
  • Read katakana in loanword chunks like コーヒー and チケット.
