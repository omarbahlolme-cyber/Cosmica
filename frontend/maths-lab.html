THE IDEA TO TAKE WITH YOU

One relationship.
Different ways to understand it.

A linear function has a constant rate of change. Its equation, graph, and table describe the same relationship. Changing one parameter changes every representation.

A straight line can model a steady rate over a particular range. Real situations also have limits; a model is useful when its assumptions fit.
