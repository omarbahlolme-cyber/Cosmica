English course FAQs

Clear answers for learners comparing study paths, levels, and lesson structure.

How should I start learning English on Cosmica?

Start with letters or pronunciation if you need sound practice, then move into vocabulary, phrases, and grammar so your English skills build in a clear order.

Is this English course for beginners or advanced learners?

Both. Cosmica supports first steps, returning learners, and advanced review by connecting core foundations with more accurate speaking, reading, and grammar practice.

Does Cosmica include audio and translation support for English?

Yes. Cosmica uses audio, translations, and guided lesson maps to help you connect pronunciation, meaning, and sentence patterns while learning English online.

What is the best next step after basic English vocabulary?

Move into phrases and grammar so you can turn single words into useful sentences, everyday conversations, and more natural English speaking.
