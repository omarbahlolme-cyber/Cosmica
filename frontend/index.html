Is Cosmica for beginners or advanced learners?

Both. Start with letters, vocabulary, and phrases, then build toward stronger pronunciation, comprehension, and advanced grammar.

Which languages can I learn on Cosmica?

English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, German, Portuguese, Dutch, and Swedish.

Does Cosmica include audio and structured lesson paths?

Yes. Each course is organized into letters, words, phrases, and grammar with audio support and guided progression.
